Our Story

The Journey

History of Miks Coffee Shop

Miks Coffee Shop got it's name from the owner, Marichris Lebrilla, who is also known as Kim. The name was created from a simple but meaningful idea, when the name "Kim" is reversed, it becomes "Mik". From this, the name Miks Coffee Shop was formed.

The word Miks also represents "mix", which reflects the variety of food and drinks that the coffee shop offers... inspired by her desire to support others and create a place where people could work together and grow.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

The mission of Mik's Coffee Shop is to provide quality coffee, delicious food, and refreshing drinks while creating a warm and welcoming place for everyone. The coffee shop aims to bring people together and offer a variety of flavors that customers can enjoy.

Our Aspiration

Our Vision

The vision of Mik's Coffee Shop is to become a well-loved coffee shop known for its quality coffee, delicious food, and affordable prices. It aims to be a place where people feel connected with one another.
